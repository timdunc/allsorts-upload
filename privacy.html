Privacy Policy

All Sorts Upload · last updated 28 September 2026

Summary

All Sorts Upload is a private desktop application with exactly one user: the owner of the All Sorts Podcast YouTube channel, who installed it on their own computer. It does not collect information about anyone else, and it does not share information with anyone.

What the application accesses

With the channel owner's permission, and using that owner's own Google Account, the application connects to Google's APIs and accesses the following, and nothing more:

  • YouTube channel data — the owner's own channel, its videos, and their metadata, in order to upload videos and confirm that an upload finished correctly.
  • YouTube Analytics data — the owner's own viewing and engagement statistics for their own videos, used to decide what to publish.

The application requests these Google API scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to publish videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly — to read back the owner's own channel and video information.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to manage the owner's own channel resources.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read the owner's own channel analytics.
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — to read the owner's own revenue analytics.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. There is no server. The application runs entirely on the owner's own computer. The information it reads from Google is held in memory and in local files on that machine, and is transmitted to no one. It is not sold, rented, published, or disclosed to any third party.

Storage and security

The application stores its OAuth credentials — a refresh token issued by Google to the channel owner — in a local file on the owner's computer. No passwords are stored. Access to the Google Account can be revoked at any time by the owner through their Google Account permissions page, which immediately and permanently ends the application's access.

Retention and deletion

Local data may be deleted at any time by the owner by removing the application and its local files. Revoking access in the Google Account permissions page removes the application's ability to reach Google's APIs.

Children

The application is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than the channel owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

All Sorts Upload's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the features described above.
  • Google user data is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and is not sold to third parties.
  • Humans do not read Google user data, except with the owner's consent, for security purposes, or as required by law.
